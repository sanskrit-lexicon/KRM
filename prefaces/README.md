# KRM front matter — *Kṛdantarūpamālā* prefaces

OCR of the **front matter** (title pages, general prefaces, prefaces, forewords, author portrait) of the *Kṛdantarūpamālā* — *A Concordance of Verbal Derivatives* — across all **five volumes** (1965–1971), published by the Samskrit Education Society, Madras. Begun by *Śāstraratnākara, Kulapati* Pt. **S. Ramasubba Sastri** and completed after his death by his pupils **V. Srivatsankacharya** and **T. K. Pranatartiharan**, under the supervision of Dr. **V. Raghavan** (Professor of Sanskrit, University of Madras).

Source scans: Cologne Digital Sanskrit Lexicon (CDSL) csldoc build —
`https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/csldev/csldoc/build/dictionaries/prefaces/krmpref.html`

**Source language: English.** Two pages are in Sanskrit and kept verbatim (Devanāgarī): the Sanskrit title page of vol. 1 ([krmpref02.md](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/KRM/blob/main/prefaces/krmpref02.md)) and the author-portrait caption/verse of vol. 5 ([krmpref24.md](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/KRM/blob/main/prefaces/krmpref24.md)). Because the source is English, there is **no separate `.en` per-page edition** — the base `.md` files are the English text. Russian translations (`.ru.md`) are provided for every page.

The tiny running digitizer header/footer stamps and library stamps (e.g. "Indologisches Seminar der Universität Bonn") are **not** part of the original and were omitted.

|---|---|

| `build_combined.py` | Reproducible builder for the `*_all.*` files (`DICT=krm python build_combined.py`) |

## Signatures & dates found

- **C. P. Ramaswami Aiyar** — General Preface (vol. 1), *President*.
- **V. Raghavan** — Prefaces of vols. 1, 2, 3 (signed 17-4-1971 in vol. 5 too); General/Foreword references throughout.
- **K. Balasubrahmania Iyer** — Forewords, vol. 3 (Luz, Mylapore, 23-5-1967) and vol. 4 (18 July 1968), *President*.
- **N. Raghunathan** — Foreword, vol. 5 (May 1971), *President of the Society*.
- **V. Raghavan** — Preface, vol. 5 (Madras, 17-4-1971).
- Volume imprint dates: vol. 1 = 1965, vol. 2 = 1966, vol. 3 = 1967, vol. 4 = 1968, vol. 5 = 1971.

## Notes

- Sanskrit terms kept in IAST with diacritics; Devanāgarī kept verbatim where legible.
- Page 24 (vol. 5) is the author's photographic portrait, with a Devanāgarī caption and a benedictory verse, both transcribed and glossed.
- Russian translations follow CDSL conventions: personal names in Cyrillic (Рагхаван, Рамасвами Айяр, Шриватсанкачарья, Пранатартихаран, Рамасубба Шастри/Шастригал, Уитни, Чандрашекхарендра Сарасвати), bibliographic work-titles left in their original script.
