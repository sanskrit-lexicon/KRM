The *Kṛdanta* form is more popular in use than the corresponding verbal forms. It sometimes also gives elegance and smooth running to the Samskrit style. For example Bhasa in the *Pratimanataka* uses the *Kṛdanta* form instead of the verbal forms in the following verse:

सूर्य इव गतो रामः सूर्यैे दिवस इव लक्ष्मणोऽनुगतः ।
सूर्यदिवसावसाने छायेव न दृश्यते सीता ॥

I have quoted this verse to show how the *Kṛdanta* form is more popularly used by scholars and poets.
