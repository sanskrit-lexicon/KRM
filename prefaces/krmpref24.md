# Picture of the Author

*[Photograph: portrait of the author, Pt. S. Ramasubrahmanya Sastri, seated.]*

Caption above the portrait:

शास्त्ररत्नाकर कुलपति वैयाकरणसार्वभौम
ब्रह्मश्री पण्डित श्र॰ रामसुब्रह्मण्यशास्त्रिवर्याः

(*Śāstraratnākara, Kulapati, Vaiyākaraṇa-sārvabhauma, Brahmaśrī Paṇḍita S. Rāmasubrahmaṇya-śāstri-varyaḥ*)

Verse below the portrait:

श्रीरामसुब्रह्मशास्त्रिप्रसृष्टमाला कृदन्तरूपाणाम् ।
तच्छिष्यैरस्माभिः प्रमथिता सुमनसां प्रीत्यै ॥

(*śrīrāmasubrahma-śāstri-prasṛṣṭamālā kṛdantarūpāṇām | tacchiṣyair asmābhiḥ pramathitā sumanasāṃ prītyai ||* — "This garland of *kṛdanta* forms, set going by Śrī Rāmasubrahma-śāstri, has been brought to completion by us, his pupils, for the delight of the good-minded.")

_Dr. Mārcis Gasūns_
